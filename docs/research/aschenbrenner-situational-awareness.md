# Aschenbrenner — Situational Awareness

## Source
Leopold Aschenbrenner (ex-OpenAI Superalignment), June 2024. ~165 pages.
- Landing page: https://situational-awareness.ai/
- Full PDF: https://situational-awareness.ai/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/situationalawareness.pdf

## Core claim
AGI by ~2027 is strikingly plausible if you trend-extrapolate three orthogonal drivers:
1. **Compute** — ~0.5 OOMs/year of effective training compute
2. **Algorithmic efficiency** — another ~0.5 OOMs/year
3. **Unhobbling** — RLHF, chain-of-thought, tool use, longer context, agentic scaffolding

Stacked, that's roughly a GPT-2 → GPT-4 sized jump *again* by 2027. From there an intelligence explosion (AGI automates AI research) → superintelligence within a further ~1 year. Then a $1T+ compute buildout, US-government "Project" takes over, geopolitical race with China becomes the defining event of the decade.

## Applicability to our bot

**Direct: none.** No prediction markets, no trading, no finance in the essay.

**Indirect, and worth remembering:**

1. **Stocks watchlist thesis.** Chapter IIIa (the trillion-dollar cluster) is the intellectual backbone of the **Power/Nuclear AI-infra utilities** bucket — `VST CCJ TLN NRG ETR` — and the nuclear pure-plays in the **Hidden balance-sheet optionality** bucket — `SMR OKLO`. If frontier training runs need gigawatt-scale power on multi-year lead times, incumbent baseload + SMR optionality is the second-derivative trade. Cite this doc if the buckets ever need justification.

2. **Polymarket adjacent markets.** These essay theses map to markets we can scan for:
   - "AGI declared by [date]?" — usually mispriced retail noise, but flagged for Strategy 2 (asymmetric)
   - Executive-order / AI-safety-legislation markets
   - TSMC / chip-export / China-invades-Taiwan markets (IIId)
   - Compute-cluster milestone markets (100k-H100 clusters, 1GW datacenter)

3. **What NOT to do.** Do not tune agent prompts around this thesis. The bot trades on wallet-level insider signal, not macro views. AGI-timeline conviction has no place in the trade decision loop — Aschenbrenner's essay is background reading for us as operators, not signal for the agent.
